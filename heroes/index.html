Concepts 5 and 6 Newest

Two full homepage concepts with the current logo, each with its own Homeowners and quote pages.

5Daylight: black hero, pointing fig leaf New

Black header and hero. The studio fig leaf's tip points at your cursor, the mouse pointer is a little fig leaf, and the car, house and boat photos show through the leaf in step with "We cover your car / home / boat / business."

6Leaf slideshow: photos slide inside a big fig leaf New

One big Fig Leaf, the logo's leaf, is the frame: photos of a car, a house and a boat slide through it, and the headline's last word changes in step: "We cover your car / home / boat."

Photo heroes A to D No fig leaf artwork

Hover, tap or tab to the car, home or boat and it comes forward, the headline word changes and its card shows. Left alone, each steps through the three by itself.

AOpening photo panels

Three tall photos side by side; the one in focus opens wide with its card.

BStacked photo bands

Three bands top to bottom; the one in focus grows tall, with a numbered rail.

CUncover lens

A black and white strip; a gold lens follows the cursor and shows it in colour.

DCard deck

A fanned deck of photo cards; the one in focus slides to the front.
