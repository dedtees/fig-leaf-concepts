Hero concepts

Four new directions for the homepage hero, without the fig leaf artwork. The headline, buttons and the interaction stay the same: hover, tap or tab to the car, home or boat and it comes forward, the headline word changes and the card for that coverage shows. Left alone, each one steps through the three by itself. Click a concept to try it on the full homepage.

AOpening photo panels Horizontal

Three tall photos side by side. The one in focus opens wide with its card; the others fold down to labelled strips.

BStacked photo bands Vertical

Three photo bands stacked top to bottom. The band in focus grows tall with its card, and a numbered rail on the side shows where you are.

CUncover lens Horizontal

One wide strip of the three photos, dimmed to black and white. A gold lens follows the cursor and shows that part in full colour, a nod to "so nothing stays hidden".

DCard deck Vertical

A fanned deck of tall photo cards. The card in focus slides to the front and shows its details; the others tuck in behind.

All four are built in the page (HTML and CSS), not baked into one image, so the photos can be swapped for the agency's own at any time.
