Homeowners Insurance

Protect your home. Preserve what matters.

If you own a home or a condo, or you rent, you need homeowners insurance. A homeowners policy gives you financial protection if your home is damaged or if someone is injured in your home or on your property.

Homeowners insurance

Choosing your coverage

The homeowners policy that you purchase determines which perils you're insured against, such as fire, wind and theft.

  • Fire
  • Wind
  • Theft

Keeping you protected

A homeowners policy generally covers:

  1. 01

    Dwelling

    The physical structure of your home.

  2. 02

    Other Structures

    Additional structures on your property, such as swimming pools and detached garages.

  3. 03

    Additional Costs

    Incurred as a result of a covered loss.

  4. 04

    Personal Property

    Your personal belongings, including clothing, furniture and electronics.Items such as jewelry, watches and fine art may need to be covered separately.

  5. 05

    Personal Liability

    Occurrences that arise from being a homeowner.
