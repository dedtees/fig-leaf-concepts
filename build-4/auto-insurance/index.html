Seven parts of a car policy

Line by line

  1. 01

    Bodily Injury Liability

    Protects you if you harm or kill someone while driving, and provides a legal defense if a lawsuit is filed against you.It covers injury to people, not your vehicle.

  2. 02

    Medical Payments (PIP)

    No-fault personal injury protection pays the medical expenses of the injured driver and passengers in your car. There may also be coverage if you’re hit by a car.

  3. 03

    Uninsured Motorists

    Pays for your injuries caused by an uninsured driver.

  4. 04

    Comprehensive Physical Damage

    Pays for damage to your car from flood, fire or animals, plus transportation expenses if your insured vehicle is stolen.

  5. 05

    Collision

    Covers your car when it collides with another vehicle or object. Collision losses are paid regardless of fault.To keep your premiums low, take the highest deductible you can afford to pay out of pocket.

  6. 06

    Property Damage Liability

    Protects you if your car damages someone else’s property, and provides a legal defense if another party sues you.

  7. 07

    Rental Car Reimbursement

    Pays car rental expenses while your car is being repaired because of an accident.
