Meet Burke

From Wall Street to Main Street

A proud native of Linden, New Jersey, and a 1999 graduate of Rowan University, I bring a unique blend of Wall Street experience, industry knowledge, and a passion for building lasting client relationships to The Fig Leaf Insurance Agency.

My professional journey began on the floor of the New York Stock Exchange, where I developed a strong foundation in financial services, relationship management, and the importance of delivering exceptional service. Trading Wall Street for Main Street, I moved into the insurance industry alongside Bryan Tomko, becoming one of the original members of the founding Fig Leaf partnership. After a brief time away, I returned to the Fig Leaf family, bringing with me the same passion and dedication that helped shape its foundation.

Today, I play a key role in overseeing the client experience, making sure every interaction reflects Fig Leaf's commitment to personalized service, responsiveness, and trusted relationships. While I primarily work remotely, I'm also known to make the rounds, staying connected with colleagues, clients, and the community.
