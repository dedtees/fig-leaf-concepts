Our story

Fig Leaf Insurance Agency was founded to give New Jersey a better alternative to high insurance costs and impersonal service. We take the time to find the best possible coverage and price while making sure every client understands what they're buying and how they're protected.

Our leadership learned an important lesson while working on the NYSE that applies to every industry, and it's the one we hold ourselves to with every client.

“Your word is your bond.”

It's why we explain before we sell, why we shop more than one carrier, and why we pick up the phone.
