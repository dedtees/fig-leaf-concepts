Getting to know Burke

QWhere are you from?
A proud native of Linden, New Jersey, and a 1999 graduate of Rowan University, Burke brings a unique blend of Wall Street experience, industry knowledge, and a passion for building lasting client relationships to The Fig Leaf Insurance Agency.
QHow did you get into insurance?
His professional journey began on the floor of the New York Stock Exchange, where he developed a strong foundation in financial services, relationship management, and the importance of delivering exceptional service. Trading Wall Street for Main Street, he transitioned into the insurance industry alongside Bryan Tomko, becoming one of the original members of the founding Fig Leaf partnership. After a brief time away, he returned to the Fig Leaf family, bringing with him the same passion and dedication that helped shape its foundation.
QWhat do you do at Fig Leaf today?
Today, he plays a key role in overseeing the client experience, ensuring that every interaction reflects Fig Leaf's commitment to personalized service, responsiveness, and trusted relationships. While he primarily works remotely, he's also known to make the rounds, staying connected with colleagues, clients, and the community.
QWhy insurance?
Proudly referring to himself as “The Insurance Guy,” he brings enthusiasm, personality, and a genuine love for what he does. For him, insurance isn’t simply about policies and coverage — it’s about people, relationships, and making sure clients feel confident that what matters most is protected.
