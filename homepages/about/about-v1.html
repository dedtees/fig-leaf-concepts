Our story

A better alternative for New Jersey

Fig Leaf Insurance Agency was founded to give New Jersey a better alternative to high insurance costs and impersonal service. We take the time to find the best possible coverage and price while making sure every client understands what they're buying and how they're protected.

Our leadership learned an important lesson while working on the NYSE that applies to every industry: your word is your bond. It's how we've run the agency from day one, from our office on Route 36 in Hazlet.
