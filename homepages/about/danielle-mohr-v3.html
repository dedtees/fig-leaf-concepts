Danielle is our Business Development Manager and a Licensed Property & Casualty Insurance Agent who is passionate about helping clients find the right coverage while making sure they understand what they're paying for and, most importantly, what their coverage actually provides.

She loves meeting new people and building genuine, lasting relationships with her clients. Danielle believes insurance should be more than just a policy: it should come with someone you know and trust.

Becoming a client means becoming a friend.

Outside of her passion for insurance, you can find her walking her dog or spending time with her daughter, who is her best friend.
